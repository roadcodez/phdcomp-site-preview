What is the difference between deleting data and destroying it?

Deleting a file or formatting a drive usually leaves the underlying data recoverable. Data destruction means the storage media is sanitized to a recognized standard or physically destroyed, so the information cannot be recovered.

Do you provide a certificate of destruction?

Yes. Every job ends with an itemized asset report and a certificate of destruction for your audit file.

Is your data destruction NIST-compliant?

Yes. Data destruction follows NIST guidelines, and we record any additional requirements your organization or state has up front.

Is your processing partner NAID certified?

Yes. Our processing partner is NAID AAA certified for secure data destruction. We can provide current certification documents for your vendor-review file.

What methods are used to destroy data?

Drives are erased with hardware and software combinations that exceed Department of Defense recommendations, and media can be crushed, degaussed or shredded. Witnessed on-site destruction is available at your facility.

Is on-site, witnessed destruction available?

Yes. Mobile destruction units can come to your location, from a single server rack to an entire data center, with a certificate of destruction issued right away.

What happens to the equipment after data destruction?

Equipment is dismantled and recycled responsibly. Items with resale value are evaluated, so your program can offset part of its own cost where possible.

Can you pick up from multiple locations?

Yes. We schedule secure collection from single offices to multi-site rollouts, and every item is counted and logged at handover.

We are unhappy with our current provider. How hard is it to switch?

Switching is simple. Send us your current quote or terms, and we scope the job, give you a clear price in writing, schedule pickup and deliver the paperwork. We handle the transition.

Why can a smaller company cost less?

A focused team carries less corporate overhead than a national provider: no layers of account management or call centers. You get the same documented process and a direct line to the person doing the job.
