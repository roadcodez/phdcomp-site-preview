Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and what we do with it. Short and plain.

Effective November 20, 2026. This page explains what PHD Computing collects through this website, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

Only what you type into the quote form: your name, work email, phone (optional), company (optional), state (optional), the type of inquiry, and your message. If you email or call us, we keep that correspondence.

What we do not collect

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising trackers, and loads no third-party tracking scripts. Your browser stores one small note on your own device, saying you have seen our privacy notice, so we do not show it again. It is never sent to us.

How we use it

To answer your request, prepare a quote, and manage the work if you hire us. We do not sell your information, rent it, or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Quote requests are saved in our own customer records system and read by Paul and the PHD Computing team. When a job requires it, we share only what is needed to carry it out, such as pickup details, with our processing partner. Our web host and email provider handle technical data such as IP addresses in the ordinary course of delivering the site and email.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed to serve you, keep the job records and certificates you may need for audits, and meet legal obligations. Ask us at any time and we will delete what we are not required to keep.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing Paul@phdcomp.com or calling (508) 479-0248.

Security

We limit access to customer records and keep backups. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send passwords or sensitive personal data through the quote form.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and its date. Questions: Paul@phdcomp.com, (508) 479-0248.
