Our approach

Destruction you can prove

Formatting a drive or deleting files usually leaves data recoverable. We sanitize storage media to a recognized standard, or destroy it physically, to match your policy and your state's rules.

  • NIST-compliant data destruction
  • Hard drives, SSDs, servers, laptops, phones and other storage media
  • Itemized serial-number inventory for every job
  • Certificate of destruction issued per job
  • NAID AAA certified processing partner
  • Erasure that exceeds Department of Defense recommendations; crushing, degaussing and shredding available
  • Witnessed on-site destruction with mobile units, nationwide
  • Full chain-of-custody documentation
  • Customer-specific requirements recorded and followed
  • Records retained and available for your audits
